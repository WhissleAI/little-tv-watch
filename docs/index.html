TV Watch for Little

Arc listens to the game and the app reads the broadcast's own scoreboard. You get live captions, the real score, and every scoring play called with who made it.

Recorded live in the Little simulator. The viewer's voice is synthesized (Cartesia). Footage: Premier League, Arsenal v Bournemouth, demo use only.

What it does

  • Live captionsPlayers and teams are highlighted as they're named.
  • The real scoreScore and clock are read from the broadcast's scoreboard.
  • Every scoring playBaskets, goals, touchdowns and wickets are called with the player who made them.
  • Answers by voiceAsk the TV "who has scored the most?" or "what did I miss?"
  • HighlightsBig moments are collected automatically, ready to replay.
  • Watchlist"Tell me whenever Wembanyama is mentioned."
